Universal Symposium Code of Conduct

  • College ID: All participants must present a valid college identity card or bonafide certificate at the registration desk.
  • Punctuality: Teams must report to their respective venues at least 15 minutes prior to event commencement.
  • Cross-College Teams: Inter-college team formation is permitted unless specifically restricted in an event's rulebook.
  • Decisions: The decision of the judging panel and event convenors will be final and binding.
  • Academic Integrity: Plagiarism or unauthorized external assistance during live coding or presentation rounds results in immediate disqualification.
